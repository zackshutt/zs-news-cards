# ZS news-card design lessons (Zack's corrections; the scan, the 9pm lab and the optimizer read this with hit-patterns section 4)

- 2026-11-25: Zack: "Getting our asses kicked on engagement. You gotta figure out how to source images of people or other stuff to use in design." 4 of the 6 FB bangers are a famous face filling the frame, and cloud runs had no faces to use, so face stories fell back to hardware or a logo. New sourcing order for the photo area, every card, every run (tools in the repo, `zackshutt/zs-news-cards/tools/`):
  1. **People: the face library first.** `python3 tools/faces.py find "<name>"`. If it has a shot, `python3 tools/faces.py bg <slug> --fmt fb --side left --out bg-fb.jpg` (and `--fmt square` for X), then use the printed `protect` box in the spec. The library is filled by Zack's team: photos dropped in the Google Drive folder **ZS Card Faces** (https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NYZZh_BaAxgaLoj9E9jcXitydHSM7OtV), named `<person>__<mood>.jpg` (e.g. `jensen-huang__smiling.jpg`). At the start of STEP 4, list that folder with the Drive connector (search `parentId = '1NYZZh_BaAxgaLoj9E9jcXitydHSM7OtV'`), download any file not already in `assets/faces/index.json` (the `src` field) to a fresh folder, run `python3 tools/faces.py sync <folder>`, and commit `assets/faces/` with seen.json. REJECT lines (too small, no face, blurry) go in the pitch so Zack can swap the photo. Pick the mood that fits the take: smiling for a win or a flex, serious for a villain or bad news, reacting for an admission. The scan never pulls a real person's photo off the web itself. If the person has no shot, build the logo / hardware / scene version and put "Face version ready the moment <name> is in ZS Card Faces" in the pitch. `python3 tools/faces.py missing` shows who still needs photos (priority list in `assets/faces/people.json`).
  2. **Games: official Steam art.** `python3 tools/imgsource.py steam "<game>" --out DIR` gives the 1920px library hero, the store background and full-size screenshots. Pick the shot with the one moment or character a fan recognizes, never a menu or UI screen.
  3. **Hardware, consoles, products, platform news: the official newsroom or blog image.** `python3 tools/imgsource.py og <official article URL> --out DIR` (nvidianews.nvidia.com, nvidia.com/geforce/news, news.xbox.com, blog.playstation.com, nintendo.com, blogs.windows.com, openai.com/news, rockstargames.com/newswire are all reachable from cloud runs). Use the article page for the story, not the newsroom home page.
  4. **Brand mark on its color** for archetype C stories and X image 1: `python3 tools/imgsource.py logo <brand> --out mark.png` (simple-icons, 1080px, radial brand-color field).
  5. The receipt screenshot rules (layout-scan doc part 2) are unchanged for the clip and for X image 1.
  Every option still goes through the layout scan, QC gate and banger check. In the pitch, say which tier the photo came from (`vars.bg_source`: face-lib, steam-art, press-og, logo, receipt, other) so the optimizer can grade it.
- 2026-11-25: Zack on the AI "workslop" v1 card (a generic robot emoji on a purple gradient plus a mock Slack message): "Not clickbait enough you need to grab actual images that'll make it scroll stopping the robot ai background sucks think tmz." Never use an emoji, clip art or a plain gradient as the subject. Use real images: the outlet's own hero art (recolored onto a dark saturated field if it's light), the real product or brand mark, the real scene. Cloud runs can't license stock photos or pull photos of real people, so when the best image would be a real person or a stock-style photo, say so in the pitch and ask Zack for the photo (or a Dash asset).
- 2026-11-21: Zack: "in addition to the QC check you add to QC check to reference latest bangers on the platforms and make sure design text etc are setup in a way to get lots of reaction too and achieve eye catching and vitality." Every card now runs `tools/bangers.py` after the QC gate: the new post at phone size next to the current top cards per platform (scan/bangers.json, refreshed weekly by the optimizer) plus a YES/NO checklist (one giant recognizable subject, one readable receipt, headline with famous name and a number/quote/villain, a take people smash like on or argue with, something to react to in one word, same punch and contrast as the bangers). Any NO = rebuild.
- 2026-11-21: Takes. Zack rejected "Opt-in for now. Keep it that way." and "Copilot is NOT ready for my Downloads folder." and picked "Clippy walked so Copilot could read your files." - 2026-11-21: Number jokes must say what the number is. Zack didn't get "100% more players" at a glance; the fix was a Steam scoreboard (GTA 6: 0 players on Steam vs indie game: 1) with the headline "GTA 6 has 0 Steam players. This indie game has 1." Plain words over clever percentages.
- 2026-11-21: Zack on the RAM, Copilot and indie-vs-GTA6 v1 cards: "Designs kinda blow... look at the bangers on my fb that went off and understand why they did and rework." What the 1M+ cards share (KFC funeral 2.12M, Gabe 1.52M, Discord 1.33M, Nvidia SSD 1.27M, Nvidia director 1.19M, Altman RAM 990K):
  - ONE huge recognizable subject fills the whole photo area: a famous face close-up, a giant brand logo on its brand color (Discord), the real hardware shot tight and full-bleed (RTX 4090 + SSD), or the real scene (KFC coffin). Never a small product tile on a generic grid, never a blurred UI wall.
  - At most one small receipt clip tucked in a corner away from the subject, with one short highlighted line readable at phone size. No text-heavy clips, no tiny file lists.
  - Number or quote stories get a giant number treatment (Steam vs Epic $136,195 vs $916 VS split).
  - v2 fixes that matched this: RAM = full-bleed G.Skill Trident Z fan + one quote clip; Copilot = giant glowing Windows logo on Microsoft blue + the permission prompt with the key line boxed; indie = Steam scoreboard with giant 0 and 1.
- Cloud scan runs cannot pull a photo of a real public figure from the web themselves; for face stories, use the face library (2026-11-25 entry above), Zack's/Dash asset, or ask him to drop the photo in, and default to logo, hardware or scene otherwise.
- 2026-11-22 (optimizer, run 4; from the 30-day top 10 vs bottom 10 per platform and the refreshed banger set, n=20 per platform): the bangers add two patterns this doc did not have. (1) Dark or saturated field in 10 of 10 FB winners; every light or white composed background in the bottom 10 (Wii two-panel 0.32x, receipt-paper 0.26x, Siri 0.21x, PS Plus box art 0.12x) died, so a white or pastel card needs a reason. (2) On X, image 1 is the famous mark or the viewer's own hardware filling 70%+ of the frame in 8 of the top 10 (Steam logo twice, G.Skill RAM twice, Radeon, WD SSD); a wide scene photo, a bare console, fries, a key-art character, a gameplay screen or a phone UI as image 1 made the bottom 10 (9 to 56 likes), and the same KFC story did 2.13M on FB with the coffin scene and 28 likes on X with the same scene cropped as image 1. Banger check grading: 5 posts logged vars.banger_check, 5 of 5 passed first try, 0 rebuilt (n=5; all still accumulating, 4 of 5 under 40K at 24 to 45h), so the check is not yet rejecting anything and needs a harder bar on "would it stop your scroll next to these".
